Project Rationale

The true bottleneck in modern intelligence systems isn't model size—it's ownership. We are tethering our most intimate logic and daily reasoning to cloud servers we do not control.

FaraTes Electric approaches this strictly as a hardware problem. Rooted in Chennai's rigorous engineering ecosystem and driven by research in neuro-symbolic and neuromorphic architectures, this is the 3rd device. Not a smartphone app. Not a web interface.

It is a physical, edge-computing architecture designed to run high-efficiency GPTs locally in your home, drawing less power than a standard lightbulb. No internet connection required. Your data never leaves the chassis. True ambient intelligence requires absolute privacy.
